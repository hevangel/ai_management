# For a long time, we engineers think management science is just BS, who need to learn those common se

**Date:** 2026-09-15T23:11:08.690Z

**Likes:** 0 | **Comments:** 0

For a long time, we engineers think management science is just BS, who need to learn those common sense packaged in fancy business language 

Then comes AI, suddenly all those common sense turns out to be not so common if you add AI into the mix.  You have learn the management science from first principle so that you find the right way to add AI into the work force.  It makes me realise those MBA jargon actually has real useful meaning when the great mind coined them many decades ago.  We just take those terms for granted all these years
